# Code of conduct

We want participation in `mcp-config-audit` to be safe, respectful, and productive for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be welcoming and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and code, not people.
- Give and receive technical feedback with empathy.
- Respect privacy and avoid posting credentials, private configuration, or other sensitive data.
- Assume good intent while being clear about security risks.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized language, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, or publishing another person's private information are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, or other contributions that violate this policy, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in all project spaces and when someone publicly represents the project.
